Who Can Donate?

Basic donor eligibility criteria to safeguard both the voluntary donor and the recovering recipient:

  • Age between 18 and 65 years
  • Weight at least 50 kg (110 lbs)
  • Hemoglobin level ≥ 12.5 g/dL
  • Minimum 56 days between whole blood donations

What to Expect?

The entire clinical visit takes about 45 minutes, while the blood collection itself is under 10 minutes:

  • 1. Confidential medical questionnaire
  • 2. Quick fingerstick hemoglobin check
  • 3. Comfortable, sterile draw (8-10 mins)
  • 4. Refreshments, snacks & 15-min rest

Myths vs. Facts

Clearing common medical misconceptions regarding voluntary blood donation:

Myth: "Donating makes you physically weak."
Fact: Your body replenishes fluid volume in 24 hours and red blood cells in weeks.

Myth: "I might catch infections."
Fact: Every needle and bag is 100% sterile and used only once.
